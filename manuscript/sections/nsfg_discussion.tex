The independent US survey supports a measurement argument, not validation of Chinese GBD estimates. Association magnitudes changed when the denominator was restricted and the outcome narrowed. Joint survey covariance avoids incorrectly treating estimates from the same respondents as independent. PCOS retained a positive narrow-outcome association; endometriosis did not show a precise protective or harmful narrow-outcome association. Age, diagnostic access, previous treatment, surgical sterilization, parity and selection into ongoing unions remain uncontrolled explanations. These are descriptive estimand changes, not causal effects.

The two data sources cannot be directly pooled or calibrated: NSFG reports diagnostic history and fertility problems in US households; GBD models cause-specific infertility in all-female population denominators. We have not established whether NSFG evidence contributed to upstream GBD modeling. Independent therefore means separately acquired records and independently coded calculations, not a proven held-out GBD validation sample. The survey does not corroborate the Chinese endometriosis terminal-year jump or numerical GBD composition shares.
